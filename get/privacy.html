Privacy

Last updated: October 29, 2026. BizzyBee is a free beta made by one indie developer.

Short version: your progress is saved to your account, encrypted, so it's safe if you change phones. Nothing is sold, there are no ads and no trackers. Other online features are optional, and each one sends only what it needs.

Your account and save

  • Your email and password sign you in (Netlify Identity).
  • Your habits, dailies, to-dos, quests, notes, bees and progress are saved on your phone and to your account. The cloud copy is encrypted, and backups are kept so you can restore one.
  • Private notes you lock are encrypted with your own passcode. Nobody else can read them, including the creator.
  • The creator can't open your save. Only if you tick Attach my save in Contact support is a copy sent, so the creator can see what went wrong.

Only if you turn it on

  • Reminders. To send a reminder when the app is closed, the times and titles of upcoming reminders and your device's push address are stored on the server.
  • Weather. Your approximate area, or a city you type, is sent to Open-Meteo to get the forecast. It isn't stored by BizzyBee.
  • Steps (Android). Only today's step count is read from Health Connect. No other health data.
  • Phone calendar (Android). Events are read to show them in Calendar, and To-Do due dates can be added to your calendar. They stay on your phone.
  • Face or fingerprint unlock. Handled by your phone. BizzyBee never sees it.
  • Friends' hives. Sharing your hive code publishes a summary of your hive (name, level and bees) so people with the code can visit. Turn it off to remove it.
  • Mailbox. Your 👍 or 👎 on a letter is stored so the creator sees totals.
  • Invites, feedback and Contact support. What you type in those forms, your email, and (for support) your app version and phone type are sent to the creator. A screenshot or save copy is sent only if you add it.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no ad trackers, no selling or sharing your data.
  • No reading of your contacts, messages or photos, unless you pick a photo yourself.

Delete your data

  • On the phone: uninstall the app or clear the site's data.
  • Online: email leoswagedout@gmail.com from your account's email and your account, cloud saves and backups will be deleted.
