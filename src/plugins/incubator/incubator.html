Incubators

Bought with Honey coins. One works at a time. A better one replaces the old one, and you keep the ones you own.

How it works
  • Eggs take 7 days to hatch. A working Nurse takes 1 day off.
  • Every egg inside loses 1 day each time you reach the step goal.
  • Every 4 finished dailies count as 1 day off too, so it works on the website.
  • Your own step goal (set it under Step stats) gives XP, a little Fitness and Health time, and a random small reward.
  • There's a daily cap, so one long walk can't hatch everything.
  • Eggs keep their secrets. Rarity and job stay hidden until they hatch.
