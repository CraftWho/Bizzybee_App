⚔️ HIVE DEFENSE

Practice mode. Your bees fight, but battles don't change your hive yet.

How to play Tap a bee: blue = move, red = sting, green = heal. You can sting and then keep moving with what's left. Tap the bee again to finish it. Hills go up to 3 floors: climbing costs 1 more, and you can only step up 1 floor at a time. From higher ground you hit harder, and ranged bees reach 1 further. 🍯 Hive points: end a turn on one to claim it (+3 honey a turn). Tap your 🏠 base to call bees with honey. Win by taking the wasp nest 🪺 or clearing every raider. Lose if they take your base. Your squad comes from your hive: Guardian, Scout, Nurse and Barrier talents fight as themselves, and Collectors are Snipers.
