Special items

Egg Incubators

Paid with Honey coins. Eggs inside hatch faster as you walk or finish dailies. One works at a time, and a better one takes over. Open your incubator

Items bees hold

Rare and Epic bees hold 1 item, Legendary and Named hold 2. Use-up items fire on their own when needed. Gear stays on. Give one from a bee's card on Hive Bees.

Trait gear

Each item gives a bee a trait, and a Common or Rare bee a shot at Epic. Use them from a bee's card on the Hive Bees page.
