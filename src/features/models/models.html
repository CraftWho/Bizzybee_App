🧬 BEE MODELS

Every rarity and element, seen at a size that's actually easy to look at - not shrunk down for a card or an icon.

The bee everywhere else in the hive (the FAB, your cards, the Queen) is deliberately tiny - it has to work at 26px just as well as 400px. This page exists to build and check the natural form first, full size, before it gets shrunk down for everywhere else.

The plan is for every entry below to eventually be a real 3D model you can rotate and zoom, the way Fallout 4 lets you turn an item over in your hands. None of that exists yet - what you're looking at is still the flat NanoBee art, just given real room to breathe. Click any bee to open it up bigger and try the rotate/zoom preview; it's a stand-in for the real thing, not the real thing.
