🎒 INVENTORY

Everything your Collector bees can bring back, plus your full colony roster.

🌾 Materials

Collector bees have a chance to bring one of these back whenever you complete a task or daily. Quests award more too - bigger DP and faster completion times both mean bigger hauls. High-grade materials unlock cosmetics down the line.

🧬 Evolution Items

Rare finds from completing quests. Use them from a bee's card in the roster above - an Evolution Stone guarantees a bee evolves up one rarity tier and is assembled from 5 fragments; a Rebirth Stone is the single rarest find and guarantees a bee jumps straight to Legendary; Drill Hands and Nanite Bolts are more common and give an Epic+ bee a chance at a permanent trait.

🎁 Store Items

Bought at the Honey Store with materials. Trait items work on any bee that isn't already a Named Beast, and also carry a chance to bump a Common/Rare bee straight to Epic. The Identity Card guarantees a bee becomes a full Named Beast - but only if you haven't personally renamed it. Use them from a bee's card in the roster above.
