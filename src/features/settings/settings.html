⚙️ SETTINGS

🐝 Eevee

Talk to Eevee from the honeycomb button anywhere in the app. Here, you can export what she's picked up so far. See ROADMAP.md's "Two-track plan" for what these exports are actually for.

Export Conversations

Downloads everything you and Eevee have said to each other as a .jsonl file - the raw material for training her for real, later.

Export Quest Data

Downloads your quest and career history as its own .json file, kept separate from conversations since it's a different shape of data.

💾 Data Management

Your progress lives in this browser. Use these if you've hand-edited the blueprint file and want those changes reflected here.

Sync from Blueprint

Pulls in content changes (names, objectives, new or removed quests/tasks) while keeping your progress - completed tasks and your level are untouched.

Reset Local Data

Wipes everything and reloads fresh from the blueprint. This cannot be undone - all progress will be lost.

📱 Full Save Transfer

Move your entire progress - every quest, DP, level, bee, everything - to another device. No account or internet connection needed, just a file you carry over yourself.

Export Full Save

Downloads your complete Vault as one file. Send it to your other device however you like (email, Drive, AirDrop) and import it there.

Import Full Save

Loads a save file exported from another device. This replaces everything currently in this browser - make sure you actually want to overwrite it first.

🔐 Session

Log Out

Clears this device's login and sends you back to the login screen.
