Bee Cards · v2.4

This version goes back to the dark card body. The rarity color now lives in the art window, the aura behind the bee and the border glow. The rarity tag and name share one line. Stats are replaced with abilities, like Pokémon attacks or Yu-Gi-Oh effect text. The bee's gold parts can now take on the rarity or element color. Tap a card to flip it. Review only. Nothing in the app has changed.
