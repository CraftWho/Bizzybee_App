BizzyBee privacy

BizzyBee is a habit and task tracker. Your save lives on your phone and, if you sign in, in your own encrypted cloud save.

Steps (Health Connect)

  • BizzyBee reads only your step count. It never reads any other health data and never writes to Health Connect.
  • Steps are used to finish step dailies and give Fitness XP. The daily totals are kept in your save.
  • Steps are never sold, shared, or used for ads.
  • Turn it off any time in Health Connect › App permissions › BizzyBee.

Location

Only your approximate area, only for the weather, only while the app is open. You can type a city instead.

Camera and photos

Only the photo you take or pick is used. BizzyBee can't see the rest of your photos.

Face and fingerprint

Your phone checks it. BizzyBee never sees or stores it.
